UI Design

Malbelshop

A design of an e-commerce website for local small businesses, focusing on improving the user experience and increasing conversion rates.

Role
UI Designer
Tools
Figma
Project Type
Personal Project

Problem Statement

Many local small businesses (UMKM) still rely on offline sales or social media to sell their products, which can limit their reach and make purchasing less convenient for customers. Without a dedicated website, it can also be difficult to organize product information and streamline the buying process.

Design Goals

  • Create a simple e-commerce interface for a local UMKM business.
  • Help customers easily browse products and make purchases online.
  • Present different product categories clearly, including clothing, snacks, frozen food, and fruits.
  • Design a straightforward checkout flow to support quick purchases.

Design Process

ResearchObserved common structures used in small e-commerce websites and analyzed how product information and checkout flows are typically organized.
WireframesCreated quick wireframes focusing on three main pages: the landing page, product view, and checkout flow.
UI ExplorationDesigned the interface in Figma with a simple and friendly layout that highlights product categories and supports easy navigation.
Design DecisionsFocused on the most essential pages needed for a small online store: a landing page for product discovery, a product view page for detailed information, and a checkout page for completing purchases.

Final Design

Key Takeaways

This project helped me practice designing a simple e-commerce interface within a short timeframe. I focused on prioritizing essential pages and creating a clear shopping flow for users.
